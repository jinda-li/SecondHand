二手清单 · 更新于 2026 年 10 月 22 日 · English

这是什么

二手清单是一个免费的个人工具：帮你整理要出的二手物品，生成可以分享的链接，并记录谁预订了哪件。它不是交易平台，不参与买卖，不处理付款。

你的责任

  • 只发布你自己的物品，内容真实，不发布违法或违禁物品。
  • 价格、取货时间和地点由买卖双方自行沟通，交易风险由双方自行承担。
  • 物品的自动翻译可能有错，以卖家的原文为准。
  • 保管好你的管理链接，拿到它的人可以修改你的清单。

我们的权利

我们可能删除违规内容，或在不另行通知的情况下修改、暂停这项服务。服务按“现状”提供，不对可用性或交易结果作任何保证。

Secondhand List · updated 22 October 2026

What this is

Secondhand List is a free personal tool: it helps you organise the secondhand things you are selling, makes a link to share, and records who reserved what. It is not a marketplace, takes no part in sales and handles no payments.

Your responsibilities

  • List only your own things, describe them truthfully, and list nothing illegal or prohibited.
  • Buyers and sellers agree on prices, times and places between themselves and bear the risks of the deal.
  • Automatic translations of items can be wrong; the seller's original text is what counts.
  • Keep your management link safe: anyone who has it can change your list.

Our rights

We may remove content that breaks these terms, and may change or suspend the service without notice. The service is provided "as is", with no guarantee of availability or of any deal's outcome.
