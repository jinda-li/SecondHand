隐私政策

二手清单 · 更新于 2026 年 10 月 26 日 · English

二手清单是一个个人二手物品清单与预订工具：卖家上架自己的物品，买家点开链接排队预订。我们只收集让这件事运转所需的最少信息。

我们收集什么

  • 卖家填写的内容：名字、清单标题、联系方式（微信号和/或 Telegram 用户名）、取货地点、物品照片、名字、价格和说明。
  • 买家排队时填写的内容：微信号或 Telegram 用户名。
  • 自动翻译：物品名、说明、清单标题和取货地点会由 Cloudflare Workers AI 翻译成另一种语言（中文/英文），译文和原文一起保存，卖家可以修改。
  • Google 登录（可选）：如果你选择用 Google 登录，我们会保存 Google 提供的账号标识、名字和邮箱，用来把清单关联到你的账号。我们不会读取你的 Google 通讯录、邮件或其他数据。
  • 你主动发的反馈：反馈内容、选填的联系方式，以及你当时在哪个页面、用的什么设备和浏览器。
  • 浏览器本地存储与 Cookie：用来记住你的管理权限、排队记录、联系方式、界面语言和登录状态。
  • 防滥用计数：为了防止有人刷爆服务，我们按天统计每个网络的请求次数。记下的是网络地址和日期一起算出的摘要，不保存 IP 地址本身，几天后自动删除。

谁能看到

  • 清单、物品照片、价格和排队人数对所有拿到链接的人公开。
  • 卖家的联系方式只显示给排在第 1 位的买家。
  • 排队名单（买家的微信号或 Telegram 用户名）只有卖家能看到。
  • 你的 Google 邮箱只用于登录，不会显示给其他用户。

我们不做什么

  • 不出售、不出租、不分享你的信息给第三方用于广告或营销。
  • 不处理付款，不收集任何支付信息。

存储与删除

数据存放在 Cloudflare 的服务器上。卖家可以随时下架物品或结束清单。如需删除你的清单、排队记录或账号关联，请发邮件到 jinda.li@outlook.com，我们会尽快处理。

联系我们

有任何问题，请联系 jinda.li@outlook.com。

Privacy

Secondhand List · updated 26 October 2026

Secondhand List is a tool for listing your own secondhand things and taking reservations: sellers list items, buyers open the link and get in line. We collect only what it takes to make that work.

What we collect

  • What sellers enter: name, list title, contacts (a WeChat ID and/or Telegram username), pick-up place, item photos, names, prices and details.
  • What buyers enter when reserving: a WeChat ID or Telegram username.
  • Automatic translation: item names, details, list titles and pick-up places are translated between Chinese and English by Cloudflare Workers AI; the translation is stored with the original and the seller can edit it.
  • Google sign-in (optional): if you sign in with Google, we keep the account ID, name and email Google provides, to link your lists to your account. We never read your contacts, mail or other Google data.
  • Feedback you send: the text, an optional contact, and the page, device and browser you were using.
  • Browser storage and cookies: to remember your management access, reservations, contacts, language and sign-in.
  • Abuse counters: to keep anyone from overloading the service, we count requests per network per day. What we store is a hash of the network address and the date, never the IP address itself, and it is deleted after a few days.

Who sees what

  • Lists, item photos, prices and queue sizes are visible to anyone with the link.
  • The seller's contact is shown only to the buyer who is first in line.
  • The queue (buyers' WeChat IDs or Telegram usernames) is visible only to the seller.
  • Your Google email is used only for sign-in and never shown to others.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent or share your information for advertising or marketing.
  • We don't handle payments or collect payment details.

Storage and deletion

Data is stored on Cloudflare's servers. Sellers can take items down or end a list at any time. To delete your list, reservations or account link, email jinda.li@outlook.com and we will handle it promptly.

Contact

Questions? Email jinda.li@outlook.com.
